Owner-occupied housing in price statistics

Four approaches to measuring the price development of owner-occupied housing, and why no international consensus exists. Intended as a basis for discussion.

Interactive application in two parts. Part one compares four OOH approaches through three layers (concept, data needs, consequence) with a dot matrix for suitability and a line chart of simulated inflation paths 2010-2025. Part two presents the four fundamental tensions that make OOH measurement contested.
